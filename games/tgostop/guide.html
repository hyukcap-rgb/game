Trump Go-Stop

Korea's favourite card game, 고스톱 (Go-Stop), played with an ordinary 52-card deck. Match ranks, collect sets, score 7 points first, then gamble: Go for more or Stop and win.

1. Read a card 카드 읽기

Every card tells you two things: its rank (big number in the corner) and its type (the badge on the right and the label strip at the bottom).

A
Rank = month. Go-Stop is normally played with Korean flower cards, one suit per month. Here the ranks A, 2 … 10, J, Q stand for months 1 to 12. You capture cards of the same rank — suit does not matter for matching.
B
Type = how many points it is worth. The suit shows it at a glance: ♠ Bright · ♦ Animal · ♥ Ribbon · ♣ Junk. Badges and strips mark the special ones (birds, ribbon sets, Double Junk).

2. Your turn 차례

Each player starts with 10 cards; 8 cards lie face up on the table and the rest form the draw pile. The winner of the last round goes first — against the computer too. On every turn you do two things:

1
Play a card from your hand. If a table card has the same rank, your card lands on it. Glowing cards in your hand have a match.
2
Flip the top card of the draw pile. It also lands on a matching rank, or stays on the table.
3
Take every pair you made. Captured cards go to your side, sorted by type. Then it is your opponent's turn.

How many cards of that rank are on the table?

On the tableWhat happens
NoneYour card stays on the table.
OneYou take both cards.
TwoPick which one to take (the better one, usually).
ThreeYou take all four, and also steal 1 Junk card from your opponent.

K cards are bonus cards. Play one any time: it goes straight to your pile as extra Junk, you draw a replacement, and you play again. If you flip one from the pile, keep it and flip again.

3. Scoring 점수

Points come only from the cards you have captured. Your score updates live in the game.

CollectExamplePoints

Junk counting: a Double Junk counts as 2, a bonus K as 2 or 3. The 9 of spades is special: it counts as an Animal or as a Double Junk, whichever gives you more points (the game decides for you).

4. Go or Stop 고 · 스톱

The moment you reach 7 points, you must choose.

S
Stop — the round ends now and you win your points.
G
Go — keep playing to win bigger. 1 Go adds +1 point, 2 Go adds +2, from 3 Go your total is doubled (×2, ×4, ×8 …). You may only call again after your score goes up.
The risk: if you call Go and your opponent reaches 7 and stops first, you lose double (Go-bak). If nobody can stop before the cards run out, the round is a draw (Nagari).

5. Special moves 특수 상황

These are where the big screen effects happen.

Ppuk — "stuck" 뻑

You match one table card, then the flipped card is the same rank too. All three stay stuck on the table. Whoever later plays the 4th card takes all four and steals 1 Junk (2 if it was your own Ppuk). Three cards of one rank dealt onto the table at the start also count as a Ppuk.

Jjok — "kiss" 쪽

Your played card had no match, but the flipped card matches it. Take both and steal 1 Junk.

Ttadak — "double click" 따닥

Two table cards of a rank, you play the third, and flip the fourth. Take all four and steal 1 Junk.

Sweep 쓸

You clear every card off the table. Steal 1 Junk.

Shake 흔들기

You hold three cards of one rank and none is on the table. Show them when you play one: your winnings are doubled.

Bomb 폭탄

You hold three of a rank and the fourth is on the table. Play all three at once, take all four, steal 1 Junk, double your winnings. Your next two turns are flip-only.

First Ppuk 첫뻑

Ppuk on your very first turn: collect 5× the stake immediately.

Ppuk again on your second turn: collect another 10× the stake.

Ppuk on each of your first three turns: collect 15× the stake and win the round on the spot.

Three Ppuks 3뻑

Make Ppuk three times in one round (any turns): you win instantly with 10 points.

Four of a kind 총통

You are dealt all four cards of one rank: instant win, 10 points.

6. Penalties for the loser 박

Each one doubles what the loser pays (they stack).

NameWhenEffect
Pi-bak 피박Winner scored with Junk and the loser has only 1–5 Junk.×2
Gwang-bak 광박Winner scored with Brights and the loser has no Bright.×2
Go-bak 고박Loser had called Go, then the other player won.×2
Meong-tta 멍따Winner has 7 or more Animal cards.×2
Money in this game is virtual. Gold (G), the in-game currency, stays inside the game: nothing can be bought, cashed out or gifted.

All 52 cards 카드 전체

The suit tells you the type: ♠ = the 5 Brights, ♦ = Animals, ♥ = Ribbons, ♣ = Junk (Double Junk and the four bonus Kings too). Some cards repeat — just like the matching Junk cards in Hwatu.
